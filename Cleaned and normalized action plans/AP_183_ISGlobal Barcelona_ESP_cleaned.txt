isglobal’s research assessment reform action plan 2024 - 2027 1. background isglobal approved its procedure for researchers evaluation and promotion in august 2018 including the endorsement of the san francisco declaration on research assessment (dora) and its commitment to the leiden manifesto for research metrics. the procedure includes the criteria, requirements and process to evaluate tenure and tenure-track group leaders at isglobal. since then, isglobal has had two rounds of evaluation for promotion (2020 and 2022) and after each round, the isglobal’s core scientific committee reviewed and introduced improvements to the procedure. in october 2022, isglobal signed the agreement on reforming research assessment and joined the coalition for advancing research assessment (coara), following the approval of its core scientific committee and isglobal’s directorate in september 2022. as an initial step the self-assessment template used for the evaluation of researchers was updated in september 2022 to be in line with the vision of the agreement that the assessment of researchers should recognize the diverse outputs, practices and activities that maximize the quality and impact of research, and that assessment should primarily be based on qualitative judgement, , isglobal endorsed the creation of the coara spanish national chapter in april 2023 and aims to be actively involved in its activities. the proposed action plan has been discussed with all research faculty and the core scientific committee and direction committee has discussed and approved the action plan in april 2024. 2. isglobal action plan for research assessment reform objective action indicator responsible calendar 1. 1 the core scientific committee will lead and appoint a working group ‐ the
