university of derby coalition on advancing research assessment (coara) action plan introduction the university of derby joined the coalition on advancing research assessment (coara) in june 2023. in joining coara and signing the associated agreement on reforming research assessment the university signalled both internally and externally its willingness to continue to develop approaches for responsible research assessment to support excellent, impactful research. the agreements principles and associated commitments reflect the direction in which the university is already progressing in terms of research assessment and the development of its research culture, as evidenced by the university being a signatory of the san francisco declaration on research assessment (dora) and an early adopter of more than our rank (mtor). the coara action plan therefore provides the opportunity for the university to reflect on and enhance areas of good practice, while also extending our consideration and practice to further embed the principles of responsible research assessment across the institution. the coara action plan and its implementation provides a means of giving focus particularly to aspects of the ‘principles’ section of the university’s innovation and research strategy 2024-2029 and to the university’s ref 2029 strategy and approach. the specifics of the coara action plan will evolve as the implementation of these two strategies progress, noting that they will also be influenced by external drivers, particularly the university ref strategy as further guidance for ref 2029 becomes available. this coara action plan was approved by the university’s academic board in january 2025. 1 coara internal driver action timeframe/ responsibility commitment
