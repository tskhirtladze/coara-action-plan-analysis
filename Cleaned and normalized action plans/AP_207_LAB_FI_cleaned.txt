coara action plan coara action plan lab university of applied sciences version 1.0 13.1.2025 coara action plan 1 1. introduction and purpose of the action plan objectives the coara action plan outlines the steps lab university of applied sciences will take to align its research and researcher evaluation practices with the commitments in the coalition for advancing research assessment (coara) agreement. its purpose is to establish, strengthen, and reform evaluation practices to reflect responsible evaluation principles, em- phasizing fairness, transparency, integrity, competence, and diversity. this plan is designed to create an equitable and supportive but pragmatic evaluation framework. context lab university of applied sciences signed the coara agreement at the end of 2023, proving a strategic commitment to advancing responsible research assessment. the action plan builds on lab’s strategic goals, 2024 interim strategy review and the operational changes implemented in 2024. these foundational elements provide a base for embedding coara commitments into daily activities, ensuring compliance with the agreement while establishing a professional research culture that balances organizational priorities with responsible eval- uation principles. scope of the plan the action plan spans a five-year period (2025–2029), providing a structured framework for implementing and refining research evaluation practices. the timeline includes key mile- stones for reforms, integrating coara principles into institutional operations, assessing an- nual progress and overall impact by the end of year. the remaining two years, 2028-2029, will be planned based on overall impact assessment. key focus of the action plan: • promoting researcher mobility: enhancing opportunities for outgoing and incoming col- laborations. • establishing career pathways: recognizing diverse researcher profiles and contributions. • establishing regional impact metrics: capturing the societal relevance of research activi- ties. • strengthening collaboration in networks: building interdisciplinary and inter-organiza- tional partnerships. • enhancing doctoral training programs: supporting early-career researchers with struc- tured development. coara action plan 2 • responsible researchers and research evaluation: developing processes, instructions and evaluation measures for responsible researcher and research evaluation. the coara process in lab university of applied sciences the coara process in lab follows a structured timeline to ensure the development and implementation of the coara action plan: • current state analysis (january-april 2024): a comprehensive analysis of the current research assessment practices at lab, identifying gaps and areas for improvement. • benchmark and knowledge sharing between universities in finland (march-may 2024): collaborative benchmarking with other universities in finland to share knowledge and best practices in research assessment. • plan preparation (august 2024): initial preparation of the coara action plan, outlining the necessary steps and actions to align with coara commitments. • plan review by the expertise groups in lab (september 2024): a review of the draft action plan by internal expertise groups to refine and adjust the proposed actions. • plan review by the management group of lab and formal acceptance of coara action plan (october 2024): the management board reviews the action plan and for- mally accepts it, ensuring alignment with lab's strategic goals. • plan preparation (final version, november-december 2024): final revisions and preparation of the coara action plan, ready for full implementation in 2025. 2. coara approach in lab strategy and change approach the coara reform at lab university of applied sciences is aligned with its strategic priori- ties, such as sustainability, innovation, and fostering a circular economy. these priorities emphasize regenerative growth, interdisciplinary collaboration, and creating intangible value, which guide the integration of coara commitments. the action plan integrates coara principles into everyday practices, focusing on improving research practices and adopting qualitative evaluation methods that emphasize both the quality and societal impact of research. implementation is phased over five years, with reg- ular feedback guiding adjustments. this structured approach ensures the sustainable adop- tion of coara principles while supporting lab’s commitment to innovation and fostering a fair and professional research environment. coara action plan 3 involvement of lab community active involvement of the institutional community is central to the coara action plan. re- searchers participate in initiatives such as mobility programs, career pathway development, and development of responsible researcher evaluation. these efforts support researchers at all career stages, fostering an inclusive and collaborative approach to professional growth. engagement methods include pilot projects for international mobility, workshops, and con- sultations to refine career pathways and feedback systems. regular progress updates, knowledge-sharing sessions, and peer learning opportunities ensure transparency and pro- mote good practices within and beyond lab. feedback from the institutional community is collected continuously to refine reform implementation and ensuring alignment with coara principles. lab’s collaborative approach integrates coara principles into institutional culture, not just policies. by engaging the research community and fostering open communication, lab aims to build cultural change that supports long-term institutional development. 3. current state lab university of applied sciences (lab) is a relatively new institution, established in 2020 through the merger of lahti university of applied sciences and saimaa university of applied sciences. this recent formation presents both opportunities and challenges in aligning with the coalition for advancing research assessment (coara) commitments. strategic fit and current metrics lab's strategy emphasizes innovation, business, and industry, with a strong focus on sus- tainability and the circular economy. the institution aims to serve global business through practice-oriented higher education and research, development, and innovation (rdi) activi- ties. this strategic direction aligns with coara's emphasis on responsible research assess- ment and the promotion of diverse research contributions. as a relatively new organization with a young research tradition, lab is in the process of establishing its research culture and assessment practices. this stage of development pro- vides a unique opportunity to integrate coara commitments from the outset, fostering a culture that values qualitative evaluation and diverse research outputs. coara action plan 4 traditionally, the national level metrics have emphasized financial indicators, such as the monetary value of projects, rather than the broader impact of research. this focus may over- look significant contributions that do not directly translate into immediate financial gains but are valuable in terms of societal impact and knowledge advancement, especially on the re- searcher level. this perspective is acknowledged when the action plan was created. diverse research activities the institution's researchers possess varying levels of formal education, reflecting lab's ap- plied research focus. this diversity necessitates assessment practices that recognize and value different types of expertise and contributions, moving beyond traditional academic met- rics. lab's research activities are multifaceted, encompassing various roles and expecta- tions. this complexity requires flexible assessment frameworks that can accommodate a wide range of research outputs and impacts, ensuring that all contributions are fairly evalu- ated. as a university of applied sciences in finland, lab emphasizes practical, industry-oriented research. aligning with coara commitments involves ensuring that assessment practices appropriately value applied research outcomes, such as innovations, prototypes, and indus- try collaborations, alongside traditional academic publications. 4. filling the gap between current state and coara core commitments based on the current state analysis and the commitments outlined in the coara agree- ment, specific change needs have been identified. these needs have been organized into three distinct action types: establish, reform, and review. this categorization enables a structured approach to aligning with coara principles while addressing both existing gaps and areas requiring enhancement (table 1). establish: this category focuses on initiating new structures, processes, or frameworks that do not currently exist but are crucial for meeting coara commitments. establishing these foundations is essential for addressing identified gaps in research assessment prac- tices. reform: the reform category addresses existing practices and processes that require sig- nificant modifications to align with coara commitments. this involves transitioning from outdated or inappropriate assessment methods to innovative approaches centered on qual- ity, fairness, and inclusivity. coara action plan 5 review: review actions focus on systematically assessing and iterating upon existing crite- ria, tools, and processes. this category ensures that research assessment remains rele- vant, effective, and aligned with the evolving needs of researchers and the research com- munity. based on the current state analysis and the requirements set by the coara commitments, it was identified that many of the coara requirements necessitate establish activities. this is due to the partial absence of existing tools, processes, and professional roles required to meet these commitments. the most significant reason for this gap is that the institutional system and working culture at lab are primarily rooted in education and regional develop- ment work, which traditionally prioritize different operational focuses. it was also recognized that the coara action plan should not be perceived as irrelevant or an additional layer to daily activities. to ensure engagement, it was deemed critical that employees and management at all organizational levels understand the importance and rel- evance of coara-based activities. these activities should be framed as development initi- atives the organization would need to undertake regardless, aligning with its broader goals. this approach, referred to in the lab community as the "embedded approach," ensures that changes are integrated into daily activities and result in actual, meaningful transfor- mations within regular work processes. despite the practical nature of the development actions, it is essential that all establish, re- form, and review activities are evaluated rigorously. this evaluation is conducted in align- ment with coara principles, focusing on the progress achieved compared to the baseline set in the current state analysis (2024). furthermore, the alignment of these actions with lab's strategic goals ensures that the initiatives contribute to the institution's broader mis- sion and vision. this dual focus on coara compliance and strategic fit guarantees that the development actions not only address immediate gaps but also support long-term institu- tional growth
