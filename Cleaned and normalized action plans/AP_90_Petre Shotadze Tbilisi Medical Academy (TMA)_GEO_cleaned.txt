petre shotadze tbilisi medical academy coara action plan tma, as a student-oriented higher education institution, primarily seeks to teach and develop national and international healthcare professionals in an innovative manner by using evidence-based knowledge, whilst practically benefiting the community in georgia through research-based outreach activities. introduction petre shotadze tbilisi medical academy (tma) - the university focused on quality and innovation in the field of healthcare education, with growing reputation in research and competence to initiate and realize projects and activities targeted on challenges of healthcare practice and education. tma recognizes the importance of research assessment and acknowledges that research results are diverse. they can include: scientific articles, data, software, different variants of the copyright as well as intellectual resources (i.e., young researchers trained accordingly with the international standards). the recommendations of san francisco declaration on research assessment (dora) as well as the principles of leiden manifesto have been used as the background for the development of the research assessment principles and methodologies at petre shotadze tbilisi medical academy (hereinafter – tma). research assessment at tma considers three-level evaluation: 1. institutional level – the efficacy of the research facilitation mechanisms and programmes as well as the institutional research activities are evaluated and analyzed (number of projects submitted for participation in external funding schemes / number of funded projects in external funding schemes, number of submitted joint (prepared by several researchers of tma) projects for participation in external funding schemes, number of registered patents and/or other forms of copyright). 2. programme level – the results of the internal funding schemes are analyzed (number of applications / number of funded projects / number of participant young scientists and students). 3. individual level – researchers scientific activity is evaluated through determination of the number of published scientific articles. tma signed the agreement and joined the coalition for advancing research assessment on february 28, 2023. same year the institution joins working group “responsible metrics and indicators”. setting the stage for change tma holds a set of core principles essential for nurturing an environment conducive to responsible research assessment. first and foremost, transparency is paramount. tma is unwavering in its commitment to maintaining transparency throughout all research assessment processes. the evaluation criteria and methodologies are articulated in a clear and open style, ensuring that the assessment process remains accountable and accessible to all involved parties. equity and inclusivity are the important aspects of reform efforts too. recognizing the diverse contributions of researchers, tma aims to cultivate a fair and inclusive assessment environment that values forms of research outputs, irrespective of origin or background. moreover, tma embraces a culture of continuous improvement; changes are to be introduced incrementally, with a strong emphasis on providing 1 comprehensive education and training to all participants. this approach ensures that individuals understand the rationale behind the changes and are equipped with the necessary skills to implement them efficiently, fostering a culture of collective responsibility and ownership. regular assessment of the progress, identification of challenges, making of informed decisions regarding the implementation of reforms are to be implemented and to ensure that process remains dynamic and responsive to evolving needs. engagement of all participants (departments and faculty members) is fundamental to success of reform efforts, with opportunities provided for input and feedback at every stage. this inclusive approach fosters a sense of ownership and collaboration among community of tma, ensuring that reform initiatives are informed by diverse perspectives and experiences. good practices are shared internally through a variety of channels, including training sessions, seminars, and peer-to-peer learning opportunities. externally, tma engages in knowledge exchange with other institutions through conferences, webinars, and collaborative initiatives, contributing to the broader advancement of responsible research assessment practices. however, key challenges in reforming research assessment, including resistance to change, and lack of awareness or understanding of responsible assessment practices are obvious. employing targeted education and training initiatives, clear communication of objectives, and fostering a supportive research culture. moreover, the importance of ongoing evaluation and adjustment of tma efforts, with alternative strategies such as pilots and mentorship opportunities being considered to overcome challenges and drive progress. through these concerted efforts, tma endeavors to establish themselve as an institution of responsible research assessment, fostering a culture of excellence, equity, and inclusivity in the pursuit of knowledge and innovation. coara commitments and how tma fulfills them reflection point description milestone facilitating recognition of tma recognizes the
