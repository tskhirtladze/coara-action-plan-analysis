processes through which research, researchers and research organisations are evaluated. the università del piemonte orientale (upo) joined coara in 2023 and, by signing arra, committed itself to pursuing, while respecting its autonomy, a common stra- 3 tegy aimed at a systemic reform of research assessment and to facilitating informa- tion exchange and mutual learning among all participants.
