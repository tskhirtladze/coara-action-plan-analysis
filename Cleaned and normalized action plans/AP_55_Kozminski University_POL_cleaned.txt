the approval process for the action plan was comprehensive and multi-phased. initially, consultations were held with representatives from various disciplines. subsequently, the plan was presented to the heads of the discipline councils. following their review and feedback, the action plan was submitted to the steering committee. upon receiving approval from the steering committee, the action plan
