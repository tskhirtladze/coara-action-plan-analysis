action plan supporting coara 2024 preamble taighde éireann – research ireland (research ireland) is the new national research and innovation (r&i) funding agency, established through the amalgamation of the activities and functions of science foundation ireland (sfi) and the irish research council (irc). whilst the action plan supporting coara 2024 reflects a consolidated position, the intent is to adopt a more harmonised approach as the new agency matures and adopts its new corporate plan to reflect the legislative position upon which it has been established1. all functions and agreements attributed to the two legacy organisations have transferred over to research ireland as of 1 august 2024. with reference to the commitments made by the legacy organisations, research ireland is a member of the coalition for advancing research assessment (coara) and remains committed to contributing to and promoting the objectives of the coalition and helping to advance the reform of research assessment (rra) in the irish research and innovation ecosystem, as demonstrated through the actions described in this plan. about research ireland research ireland is a new, competitive research and innovation funding agency in ireland. the establishment of the agency was a key action of impact 2030: ireland’s research and innovation strategy2. research ireland was established on 1st august 2024, following an amalgamation of science foundation ireland (sfi) and the irish research council (irc). research ireland is an agency of the department of further and higher education, research innovation and science (dfheris) working in collaboration with higher education institutions and other state agencies such as the higher education authority (hea), ida ireland, and enterprise ireland. 1 research and innovation act 2024 2 impact 2030: ireland’s research and innovation strategy 1 the objectives of research ireland, as set out in the research and innovation bill 2024 are to: • promote the attainment and maintenance of excellence in the standard and quality of research and innovation. • support the undertaking of research and innovation in all fields of activity and disciplines by researchers with different levels of knowledge, experience and specialist skills in such fields or disciplines. • promote and support the contribution made by research and innovation to economic, social, cultural, and environmental development and sustainability in the state. • strengthen the engagement of the research and innovation system with: o the government, ministers of the government and bodies (whether statutory or otherwise) which are funded wholly or partly by public moneys, and o enterprise, non-governmental organisations, cultural institutions and society generally; to promote and develop the reputation of the state internationally as a location that is favourable for undertaking research and innovation; to advance the principles of equality, diversity and inclusion with regard to opportunities to undertake research and innovation and in the undertaking of that research and innovation. action plan context research assessment is central to research ireland’s activities in supporting programmes of research and innovation, and education and public engagement which are both excellent and impactful. research ireland is guided by a number of organisational and national strategies which inform the organisation’s activities in this regard: - impact 2030: ireland’s research and innovation strategy - research ireland’s external equality, diversity, and inclusion (edi) strategy 2023-2028 - higher education authority act 2022 - researcher career development framework | irish universities association involvement of the research community research ireland continuously reviews its processes and policies through iterative national and international stakeholder engagement, reflecting its commitment to improving all dimensions of research culture, including equality, diversity, and inclusion (edi). the organisation works closely with the research offices of the higher education institutions, applicants, grant holders, and reviewers to ensure that research ireland effectively supports and serves the r&i sector in ireland. 2 furthermore, research ireland undertakes consultation with stakeholders beyond its traditional community, including representatives of historically underserved communities, to understand the diversity of the research community in ireland and craft interventions in rra that are actionable, impactful, and can provide the results anticipated. research ireland is also represented on a number of coara working groups, including the coara national chapter for ireland, providing the agency with diverse opportunities for innovation, consultation, and collaboration in the space of rra. key challenges and opportunities as a new organisation, research ireland has identified a number of challenges and opportunities in the rra space, building upon the journeys of the historic agencies and with reference to the new legislation upon which it has been established. therefore, the actions detailed in the below table have been considered in these contexts and its broad remit to fund across all disciplines. the actions speak to the commitments, engagements, and progress with rra attributed to both historic agencies. it is anticipated that these actions will be revisited during 2025 to ensure they are fit for purpose in the context of supporting the organisation’s remit, as well as the entire research community in ireland. a challenge concerns the effort and time burden placed on the individuals involved in the various coara working groups and forums on which research ireland sits, as well as those agency staff who directly manage research assessment processes for programme calls, including expert reviewer selection and review panel formation. meaningful contributions to the multiple coara working groups with which research ireland is associated require contributing to the formation of a broad scientific evidence base and review of international best practice in rra, the potential running of proposed pilots in research ireland programme calls, and extensive stakeholder consultation. since the actions described below in supporting rra will evolve, further resources will be required to effectively and efficiently implement changes in the organisation’s processes and policies, in line with research ireland’s commitments to coara and related initiatives such as the san francisco declaration on
